## How It Works

This is a 2-day event that takes place in the evenings.

- **Day 1:** Businesses pitch the problem they want solved and the MVP they currently have in place. Developers hear the cases and decide if they want to take the challenge. If you do, you have 24 hours to build a solution.
- **Day 2:** Developers present solutions and businesses award the winning projects.
- Multiple developers compete.
- Each business picks ONE winner.
- Winner takes home $1,000.

---

## Schedule

### Day 1 (6:00-8:00 PM)

- **Business Challenge Spotlight**
    - Featured business owners present their real challenges and product requirements
    - Business owners answer questions to define use cases and guide the build
    - Developers have 24 hours to build a solution

### Day 2 (6:00-8:00 PM)

- **Demo & Deploy**
    - Everyone demos their solutions live
    - Business owners choose ONE winning solution
    - Winner-takes-all: $1,000 prize
    - Winning solution deployed immediately

---

## Solution Philosophy

- A simple, elegant prototype meant to offer an elegant solution to the business owner.
- The solution will be deployable and usable for the business owner.
- Ongoing support and additional features will be provided.
- Upon selection, the developer will push the solution to the Organized AI repo for Project Management and ongoing Quality Assurance.

---

## Requirements

- Bring your own laptop and charger
- Solutions must be functional and deployable
- Solo developers preferred (teams welcome, but compete as one entry)
